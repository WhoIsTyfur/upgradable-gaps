# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release: put 8 gold ingots in each outer slot of a crafting table
  around a golden apple to get an enchanted golden apple.
- Server-side only, for Fabric and Quilt 1.14 - 26.3, Ornithe 1.3.1 - 1.13.2,
  Forge 1.6.1 - 26.3, NeoForge 1.20.2 - 26.3, and as a plugin for Bukkit,
  Spigot, Paper, Purpur and Folia 1.7.10 - 26.3.
